# LT Second Brain: Charter

**Purpose:** one place where the leadership team keeps what the whole team
needs in order to decide well: decisions, open asks, risks, and the
priorities we are working against. It is not a status board and not a
replacement for Slack, Jira, or meetings.

**Members (read and write):** {{members}}
**Focus owner:** {{focus-owner}} (only person who edits *LT Focus*)

## What belongs here
An item belongs only if it passes this test: *would knowing it change
someone else on the LT's decision this week?* If not, it stays in your
personal notes.

## What never belongs here
Anything about an individual's performance, compensation, health,
conduct, or career; restructuring or headcount plans before they are
announced; anything a customer or partner shared in confidence;
personal notes of any kind. These stay in each person's private layer.
When in doubt, leave it out and ask the person concerned.

## How it works
- Each of us publishes a short **Contribution** page per day (drafted by
  our own Claude from our own notes, approved by us before publishing).
- One scheduled run reads only this space and writes the **Brief**.
- **Decisions** and **Asks/Risks** get one page each, with a named owner.
- Pages have one author. Nobody edits another member's page; use a comment.
- Agents draft, humans approve. Nothing is posted outside this space
  without a person confirming it.

## Rituals
Daily: contribute (end of day), read the Brief (start of day).
Weekly: Brief in weekly mode, Friday. Focus reviewed by its owner.
